No. 01

The Mandelbrot Set

the set of c for which the orbit of 0 stays bounded

z_{n+1} = z_n² + c, z_0 = 0

Infinite structure from a single quadratic rule. Pan and zoom through the boundary in real time — Julia, Multibrot, Tricorn and Burning Ship included — with multi-precision arithmetic and perturbation theory carrying the deep dives far past where ordinary doubles collapse.

No. 02

The Lorenz Attractor

a trajectory that never repeats and never escapes

ẋ = σ(y−x), ẏ = x(ρ−z)−y, ż = xy−βz

Three simple equations for convection rolls — and the birth of chaos theory. Nearby starts diverge exponentially, yet the path is forever trapped on a butterfly-shaped attractor. A full interactive page is in the workshop.
